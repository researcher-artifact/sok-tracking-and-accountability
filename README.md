The workbook "SoK - Tracking and Accountability for Digital Objects in Information Security" is structured as a comprehensive data extraction matrix designed to support a Systematization of Knowledge (SoK) study on tracking and accountability mechanisms for digital objects in information security. It consolidates detailed information from a corpus of primary studies and organizes the extracted data into a set of methodologically‑defined dimensions.

## Dashboard

An interactive dashboard of the study (overview, data extraction form, thematic analysis, RQ1–RQ5 and heatmaps), read directly from the DEF/TA spreadsheet, is published at <https://researcher-artifact.github.io/research-artifact.github.io/>. Its source is in [researcher-artifact/research-artifact.github.io](https://github.com/researcher-artifact/research-artifact.github.io).
